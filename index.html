Research Interests

🧬

Comparative Genomics

Evolutionary studies and gene duplication to understand genomic similarities and differences across species — specializing in polyploidization, fractionation, and speciation cycles.

📐

Mathematical Modelling

Probability models and branching processes applied to evolutionary dynamics and genetic variation. Developing novel statistical frameworks for phylogenomic inference.

🤖

Machine Learning in Biology

Deep learning, CNNs, and LASSO-based methods for cancer subtype classification, protein structure prediction, and SNP analysis in large genomic datasets.

🏥

Oncology & Medical Research

Cancer genomics and predictive models for personalized medicine, including TCGA gene expression analysis and economic evaluation of health interventions.

📊

Statistical Genomics

Leveraging data mining, algorithm development, and statistical analysis to extract biological insights from high-throughput sequencing data.

🔗

Interdisciplinary Collaboration

Integrating graph theory, applied statistics, and AI tools with biology, ecology, and public health — including AI ethics in educational contexts.
